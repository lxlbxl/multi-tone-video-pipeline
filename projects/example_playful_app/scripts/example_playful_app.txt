You think you failed at every habit because you're lazy. Wrong. You failed because you were relying on motivation, and motivation always runs out by Wednesday. The people who actually stick with things aren't more disciplined than you. They just built a system that makes the habit stupidly small and impossible to forget. One push-up. One page. One check-in a day. Your brain stops negotiating when the task is that tiny, and the streak does the rest. Ninety days of "too small to skip" beats one week of "beast mode" every single time. Start with something laughably easy today, and let the streak carry you.
